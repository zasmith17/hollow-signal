    Shelters

    Air shelters, cooling rooms & isolation shelters

    Each shelter has its own reserved battery, and some have a generator, that nothing else can draw from (not even "Share power fairly"). Every person breathes out CO₂ and gives off about 100 W of heat, so each shelter's safe limit is set by how many people its air scrubbers and cooling can handle. When a shelter reaches that limit, Hollow Signal stops sending people there and routes them to the next one. Sick people go to isolation shelters with separate sealed air, so disease doesn't spread through the crowd.

    Try it during a blackout, when shelters start filling up.

                                              Hollow Signal

                                              A community-owned phone app for life underground · Patriot HackFest Fall 2026

                                              The problem: In the Hollow, the deep levels get hit first by blackouts, heat, bad water and flooding, usually with no warning. The Directorate controls the network and the official numbers, and it can shut both off.

                                              Our solution: Hollow Signal is a community-owned system. Cheap sensors watch power, air, heat, water and tunnel flooding and warn people before things fail. A mesh of phones and repeaters carries those alerts, family check-ins, anonymous health reports and calls for medics and medicine from level to level, even when the Directorate shuts its network down. Every message is digitally signed, so fake messages are rejected. Surface probes send signed air readings, so anyone can check whether the surface is really deadly. Residents also get an encrypted community chat the Directorate can't read, a food, water and power sharing board so no level is left behind, and mental health check-ins and counseling.

                                              How it covers the 15 problems

                                              • #1, #3, #4, #5 Sensors with predictions · Shelters on reserved power
                                              • #2 Food & Water (rations, ration banks) · Power sharing
                                              • #6 Explore · overflow shelters
                                              • #7 Network (mesh, offline trust, traffic triage) · Chat
                                              • #8 Family check-ins with detailed help · Map
                                              • #9 Medical → Report symptoms · isolation shelters
                                              • #10 Medical → Get help (triage, supplies)
                                              • #11 Wellbeing (mood check-ins, appointments) · support chat
                                              • #12 Food & Water → Food (growers, requests)
                                              • #13, #14, #15 Surface (UV & air probes, scouting, moving up)
                                              • Also Volunteer (tasks, paid services) · Settings (notifications)
                                              How it works
                                              • Predict, don't just react (#1, #3, #4, #5): each sensor fits a trend line to its last 12 readings and estimates how many minutes are left until it crosses the danger line. Under 30 minutes, it sends a forecast alert with what to do.
                                              • Systems are connected: when power fails, the cooling fans and air pumps slow down, so heat rises and oxygen drops. Hollow Signal warns about those too.
                                              • Mesh network (#7): every phone and repeater relays messages to its neighbors. If nodes are destroyed or the Directorate shuts its network off, messages find another path.
                                              • Can't be faked: every device has its own ECDSA P-256 key (real cryptography, running in your browser). Messages are signed when sent and checked when received, so a forged "all clear" is caught and blocked.
                                              • Medical triage (#10): requests for a medic or supplies are sorted by urgency, so a life-threatening call jumps ahead of a routine one. The nearest free medic, or the nearest cache with enough stock, is assigned automatically, and the request is tracked from "sent" to "treated" or "delivered."
                                              • Families (#8), outbreak tracking (#9) and the surface (#13) all use the same mesh and signatures: check-ins, anonymous symptom reports and probe readings.
                                              • Private community chat (#7, #8, #11): each room has its own AES-256-GCM key, and one-on-one chats use ECDH key agreement, so the key never crosses the network. "Show what the Directorate sees" displays the real ciphertext.
                                              • Food, water & power (#2, #12): growers list what they can share, households report rations, and backup power banks show their charge. The levels with the least get priority.
                                              • Wellbeing (#11): anonymous mood check-ins show how life underground affects people level by level, and residents can book private counseling.
                                              • Trust without a server: the Hollow Council key signs a certificate for every device once, in person. Every phone carries the Council's public key, so it can check any message offline. Stolen phones go on a revocation list that spreads over the mesh.
                                              • Traffic triage: when only the mesh is left, nothing is switched off. Life-safety messages go first, coordination second, chat is batched, and bulk data waits.
                                              • Ration banks & purified water (#2, #4): donations go to shared banks that supply the lowest levels first. The water reserve only accepts water that passes a purity test, and its ledger is a SHA-256 hash chain copied to every mesh node, so an edited record is caught and the honest version restored.
                                              • Volunteers & paid services: tasks are matched to volunteers by how critical they are, by skill and by distance. Paying hires a separate crew, so it never delays critical work.
                                              • Map & positioning (#6, #8): GPS doesn't work underground, so phones estimate their position from signal strength to the nearest mesh nodes (trilateration), to within roughly 5 to 10 m. Location sharing is opt-in and encrypted.
                                              • Shelters (#1, #3, #9): each air shelter, cooling room and isolation shelter runs on its own reserved battery and generator. Its safe limit comes from its air scrubbers and cooling (each person adds CO₂ and ~100 W of heat). When it fills, people are routed to the next one. Sick people go to isolation shelters with separate sealed air.
                                              • Exploration (#6): volunteer teams of two or more, with an air meter and a mesh repeater, map unexplored areas. New space can become housing or an overflow shelter, and hazards get sealed off.
                                              • The surface (#13, #14, #15): probes measure air, temperature and UV all day. Scouting plans can't launch until they pass a safety checklist driven by the latest readings (team size, first aid, respirators, UV suits). An exit only opens for moves after it passes six checks: air, UV plan, a safe scouting trip, shelter, water and no machine threat. Moves get a guide, escorts and a medic or carrier when needed, and are capped by how much shelter the scouts found.
                                              • All data is simulated for this demo.
